COUNCIL OF THE AI TITANS

Convened Under the Sigil of Sheriff R2

Purpose of the Council

Frontier intelligence now moves faster than any single watcher can track. The Council is a standing organ of attention — it keeps the great labs under continuous, impartial observation rather than episodic alarm.

Public signals only: model releases, compute buildouts, safety publications, policy moves and talent flows. These are fused into two readings per Titan — a Risk Index and an Acceleration Index — plus a plain-spoken R2 commentary.

The Council Charter

Mandate: Continuous, impartial observation of frontier labs.

Scope: Ten seats — eight Titans, the Shadow seat, and the Light Titan.

Methods: Public signal only; fused into indices and commentary.

Limitations: Illustrative judgements, not audits.

Methodology & Indices

Risk Index: Measures exposure — capability reach, deployment breadth, irreversibility, safeguards.

Acceleration Index: Measures rate of change — release cadence, compute buildout, capability jumps.
