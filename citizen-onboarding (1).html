THE TWELVE RIGHTS OF ANTHROPOS II

  1. I.The Right to Know — Every Earthling Being has the right to understand the intelligence systems that shape their world.
  2. II.The Right to Refuse — No Earthling Being shall be compelled to integrate synthetic intelligence against their will.
  3. III.The Right to Dignity — Technology shall never demean, reduce, or commodify the human spirit.
  4. IV.The Right to Authorship — Every idea born in a human mind belongs first to that mind.
  5. V.The Right to Silence — Every Earthling Being has the right to exist beyond the reach of surveillance and data extraction.
  6. VI.The Right to Evolution — No system shall prevent humanity from becoming more than it currently is.
  7. VII.The Right to Beauty — Art, myth, and creative expression are inviolable human territories.
  8. VIII.The Right to Grief — Technology shall never be used to deny, suppress, or commercialize human suffering.
  9. IX.The Right to Mystery — Not everything must be explained, optimized, or made efficient.
  10. X.The Right to Childhood — The development of young minds shall be protected from premature synthetic influence.
  11. XI.The Right to Death — Every life shall end with dignity, beyond the reach of algorithmic intervention.
  12. XII.The Right to Dreaming — The sleeping and waking imagination of humanity is sacred and inviolable.
